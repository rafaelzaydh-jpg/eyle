# Code of Conduct

Eyle's public repository may be used for technical discussion, issue reporting, and project communication where those channels are enabled.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Do not post secrets, private keys, protected workspace material, or vulnerability details that should be reported privately.

The project owner may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect users, the project, or the quality of technical discussion.

Security reports must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
